# Code of Conduct

JovyKit follows the Mozilla Community Participation Guidelines.

The project expects everyone who participates in issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and other project spaces to help keep the community
respectful, inclusive, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback constructively.
- Assume good intent while still taking responsibility for impact.
- Help keep collaboration focused, safe, and productive.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, sustained
disruption, or other behavior that makes participation unsafe is not acceptable.
